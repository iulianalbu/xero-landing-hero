# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Teams across industries evaluating a managed service for encrypting and annotating sensitive data.

## Product Purpose
Xero makes managed data encryption feel simple, dependable, and ready for team adoption. The landing hero should communicate a clear transformation from raw data to protected data and drive visitors toward getting started.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, and quietly technical. The experience should feel trustworthy and polished without looking corporate or visually noisy.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic purple or indigo SaaS branding, color-heavy interfaces, decorative dashboard patterns, and security visuals that feel alarming or militaristic. Pink-magenta is reserved for the specified signature arc and pipeline energy.

## Design Principles
- Make a complex security promise immediately legible.
- Use one memorable visual mechanism rather than many competing effects.
- Build trust through restraint, precision, and consistent interaction feedback.
- Preserve the same composition and hierarchy across desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA where the supplied art direction permits it, retain visible keyboard focus, support browser zoom, and provide a static equivalent for reduced-motion preferences.
